Some test examples to look for isohedral or anisohedral tilings. I looked at tegula #1728474 by hand for sizes 6 to 15 and a bit of 16, for a certain subset. These are ones with sum-types (1,X,0,0,0,...,0) All of these have reflections. We vary the offset to walk around the tile. These are the results I found. "iso # stands for the criteria number for isohedral. "aniso" stands for anisohedral. X = can't tile for some reason or other. (note that some of the iso 5 types are also iso 6. I'm not sure if these can all be achieved with vertex to vertex. We might have to go to midpoints of I curves. All the examples I looked at don’t need to. Some of them required to use iso 6 to be vertex to vertex. But it’s possible.)
