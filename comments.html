How to use the app.

There are several ways to use the app.
-Copy a given 2-isohedral, monohedral tiling. (Usually copying one of Joseph Myer's polyforms, But Escher did one example.)
-Look at example tilings in the gallery.
-Make up tilings on your own:
--Explore by computer
--Explore manually.
-Given a tiling by any of the above methods, change the curves to look like an outline of something in the style of Escher. (This app doesn't support drawing inside of tiles, but can export to PNG or SVG.)
